# SLA assignment, uptime by period and incident response with RCA (REQ-BSM-006, REQ-BSM-008, ADR-024)

Captured 2026-11-05 on the dev compose stack with the synthetic estate (scale 0.05, 15 business services with a
replayed health history).

## What changed on screen

- **Business services.** Every service row shows the SLA it is held to (a select for operators, with every SLA of the
  catalogue), its availability over the SLA's window ("99.2% this month") in the met/missed tone, the error budget left
  with the burn rate, and links to the new service detail route.
- **Uptime.** One table for every service: a column per month, quarter or year (segmented control), the target, the
  figure in the met or missed tone with a "Missed" tag, the current period marked "to date", and "Export CSV", which
  downloads the same report from `availabilityReportCsv`.
- **Service level agreements.** The catalogue with name, target over window, response and resolution targets for the
  critical, high and medium severities, and the number of services held to each; operators create, edit and delete.
- **Incidents.** Each card carries severity, state, source, SLA, the respond-by and resolve-by deadlines (with "Response
  target missed" / "Resolution target missed" once the engine stamps them), the assignee, acknowledge / assign /
  resolve-with-note / close actions, the RCA section (summary, root cause CI prefilled from the incident's root cause,
  contributing factors, corrective actions with owner, due date and done, RCA owner, due and completion dates) and the
  timeline that merges the audit log's entries (actor, mutation) with the engine's events. "Show resolved too" lists
  the closed history.
- **Service detail (`/services/[id]`).** Health, SLA, target, the window figure and budget bar, root causes, the uptime
  table of that service and every incident of it with the same actions.

## The 300-service measurement

The dev estate has 16 business services. For the budget, 284 more were seeded as a perf fixture (`Perf service 001…284`,
tier 3, 99.5% target), `synth-estate health-history` drew their 14-month history (3,942 outages, 2,329 down) and
`replay-history` put it through the outbox: 7,884 health events, 3,942 resolved incidents, 8,282 history rows in all.
`k6 run k6/availability-report.js` (granularity year, 3 periods, 4 VUs, 30 s) then measured 2,279 iterations with
p95 629 ms and no failed request; `tools/perf/results/availability-report.json` holds the summary guard reads. The
fixture was removed from Postgres and ClickHouse afterwards, so the screens and screenshots show the 16-service estate.
